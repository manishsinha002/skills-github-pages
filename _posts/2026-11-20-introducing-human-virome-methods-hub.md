Welcome to the **Human Virome Methods Hub**—a living, evidence-linked resource designed to track benchmarks, specimen compatibility, and validated workflows for human virome research.

## Why a Living Hub?
Unlike static software lists, every method entry here is backed by structured metadata, automated CSV schema validation, and peer-reviewed benchmark references.

## Current Starter Methods
We are launching with three core tools:
* **geNomad:** Virus and plasmid identification using hybrid marker and neural network approaches.
* **CheckV:** Quality assessment and completeness estimation for viral genomes.
* **Phanta:** Gut microbiome profiling that quantifies both prokaryotes and viruses simultaneously.

## How to Contribute
Want to propose a new method or benchmark dataset? Check out our **Contribution Guidelines** or open a pull request using our repository's issue templates.
